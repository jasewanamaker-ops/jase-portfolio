JASE WANAMAKER

Sports Play-by-Play

Introduction

I am a professional drag racer, which is where my first interest in broadcasting came from when I started announcing races at the age of 10 while also racing. I have been announcing for the past 9 years at race tracks such as U.S. 131 Motorsports Park and Bristol Dragway, and many others across 6 different states. This passion was transferred over to basketball in the fall of 2026 when I made my Analyst debut for TKDS Sports, cementing my desire to have a career in play-by-play commentary. My desire is to end up as a play-by-play commentator for ESPN doing College Football at the absolute highest level.

My approach

That there can be excitement and interest in every individual moment, the moments always feel bigger with a powerful voice behind them. I want to be entertaining for everybody to listen to and make it so that they don't have to be watching the game to understand what is happening through the picture I paint for them

Who I serve

I want to serve the fan, making a game exciting and informational. I want to add the feel that they're in the game and just hearing my voice on the broadcast makes it a better game

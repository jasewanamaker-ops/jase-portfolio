CONTACT + BOO BANGER VIDEO UPDATE

1. Unzip this package.
2. Open your GitHub repository:
   https://github.com/jasewanamaker-ops/jase-portfolio
3. Click Add file > Upload files.
4. Drag these FOUR files onto the upload page:
   app.js
   content.js
   racing.html
   styles.css
5. Also drag the WHOLE assets folder from this update onto the upload page.
   Keep the folder intact. It should upload as:
   assets/boo-banger-kilkare.mp4
   assets/boo-banger-poster.jpg
6. Do not upload this instructions file or the ZIP itself.
7. Click Commit changes and confirm. GitHub replaces the four matching files
   and adds the two new media files. Your other files and photos stay intact.
8. Give Pages a few minutes, then refresh your website.

The video appears after selecting Racing, and on the Racing page.
Contact details appear under Behind the mic > Contact.

reflection is included. Captions/transcript and your project reflection can
